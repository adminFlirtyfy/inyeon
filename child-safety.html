Child safety standards

Zero tolerance for child exploitationin any form.

InyeonAI is for adults only. This page sets out our published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), how you can report it, and what we do when we find it.

18+ only

Nobody under 18 may use InyeonAI. Accounts we find belonging to minors are deleted immediately.

Permanent ban

Any CSAE content or behaviour ends in an immediate, permanent ban. There are no warnings and no appeals.

Reported to authorities

We report confirmed incidents to NCMEC and relevant law enforcement, without exception.

Easy to report

Report a user from their profile, or use Settings → Report an issue. You can also email us. Child safety reports are reviewed as a top priority.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Our Commitment

InyeonAI ("we," "our," or "us") maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in any form. To keep Google Play and our community safe, we publish these standards and enforce them without exception.

2. What Is Prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited anywhere on InyeonAI, including profiles, photos, bios and messages:

  • Any sexual or sexualised content involving a person under 18, real or depicted
  • Grooming, or any attempt to build a sexual or romantic relationship with a minor
  • Soliciting, sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM
  • Sextortion or coercing a minor to share images or take part in sexual activity
  • Trafficking of, or arranging contact with, a minor for sexual purposes
  • Creating an account while under 18, or misrepresenting your age

3. Age Requirement

InyeonAI is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We require users to confirm they are at least 18 when registering. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we discover or reasonably suspect that an account belongs to a minor, we suspend and delete it immediately.

4. How to Report a Concern

If you see anything on InyeonAI that may involve a minor or CSAE, report it straight away. There are two ways to do this inside the app:

Option 1: Report a user from their profile

  1. Open the user's profile
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) and choose Report
  3. Select a reason: Underage user (including a profile photo that shows a child), Incorrect information, Violence, Sexual content, or Scam
  4. Submit the report

Option 2: Report from Settings

  1. Go to Settings → Report an issue
  2. Select Child safety concern from the issue list
  3. Describe what you saw, including the username or profile involved, and submit

Other ways to reach us

  • By email: support@inyeonai.com with the subject line "Child Safety"

Reports are confidential. The user you report is never told who reported them. Please do not share, forward or screenshot suspected CSAM. Describe what you saw and identify the profile instead, and we will handle the evidence appropriately.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. In India, you can call Childline on 1098.

5. How We Respond

  • Review: Child safety reports are prioritised above all other reports
  • Immediate removal: Offending content is removed without delay
  • Permanent ban: The account is permanently banned and cannot re-register
  • Evidence preservation: Relevant information is preserved as required by law
  • Mandatory reporting: Confirmed incidents are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to relevant law enforcement, including in India under applicable law
  • Cooperation: We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations

6. Prevention

  • An 18+ age requirement at sign-up and a prohibition on minors in our Terms
  • Profile photos and bios are subject to review and user reporting
  • Users can block and report any other user at any time, from a profile or from Settings → Report an issue
  • Staff who handle safety reports are bound by strict confidentiality
  • We review and update these standards regularly

Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted and stored on users' devices, so we rely on user reports and on profile content to identify abuse. This is why reporting matters.

7. Legal Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations, including Indian law and the reporting obligations that apply to us as an online platform. Nothing in our privacy practices limits our ability to report CSAE to authorities.

8. Child Safety Contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters, including requests from regulators and law enforcement, is reachable at support@inyeonai.com. We respond to child safety concerns within 48 hours.
