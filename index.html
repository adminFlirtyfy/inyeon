Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers — no fine print.

Is InyeonAI free?+

InyeonAI is free to download and use — create your profile, take personality tests, and get matched at no cost. Core features like matching and chatting are free. We also offer optional premium subscription plans and add-ons for people who want extra features or a boosted experience, but you never need to pay to find real connection on InyeonAI.

Is InyeonAI an AI dating app? How does the AI work?+

Yes. InyeonAI is an AI-powered dating app that goes beyond swiping on photos. The AI studies your mood, your intent, your personality profile, and how you interact — then matches you with people who genuinely align with who you are, not just what you look like. It acts on what you actually want, not just what you clicked on. The more you use it, the better it understands you.

What are the free psychology tests and how do they help?+

InyeonAI includes free psychology assessments built directly into the app — including the Big Five personality model, love languages, and attachment style tests. These are tools that most people pay for or never access at all. When you complete them, the AI uses your results to understand how you think, how you love, and what kind of connection you are looking for. The tests do not just help the algorithm — they help you understand yourself first. Because the clearest path to finding the right person starts with knowing who you are.

Does the AI truly understand me?+

That is exactly what we built it to do. Most dating apps track your behavior — what you swiped, how long you looked at a photo. InyeonAI goes deeper. It reads your intent: what you are actually looking for, how you communicate, what your personality says about your compatibility. Combined with your psychology test results and mood at the time of matching, the AI builds a picture of you that no simple swipe can capture. The more you engage authentically, the more accurately it matches you.
