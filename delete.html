Account & data

Your account,your call.

You can permanently delete your InyeonAI account and data at any time, directly from the app. Here's how.

Last updated: August 14, 2026

How to delete your InyeonAI account

  1. Open the InyeonAI app and sign in.
  2. Tap your Profile icon.
  3. Tap the Settings (gear) icon.
  4. Select Account, then tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when prompted. Your account will be permanently deleted.

Once confirmed, your profile, photos, matches, and personal information are permanently removed in line with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. Some data may be retained for up to 30 days for legal or security reasons before being fully purged.

Keep in mind

  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account — you must follow the steps above first.
  • Since chats are stored locally on your device and encrypted, deleting your account removes your on-device chat history but does not affect messages a recipient may have saved outside the app before deletion.
  • If you back up your chats to Google Drive, remove that backup separately from your Google Drive settings if you'd like it deleted too.
  • InyeonAI is free to use, with no subscription to cancel.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you're just taking a break, you can also simply stop using the app without deleting your account.

Already uninstalled the app?

If you've already removed InyeonAI from your device and can't complete the in-app steps, email support@inyeonai.com from the address linked to your account and we'll take care of the deletion for you. We respond within 48 hours.
