Singles events & meetups in Pune: how to actually meet in person

Updated September 2026 · 5 min read

Matching with someone online is only half the equation — at some point, dating in Pune means actually showing up somewhere. The good news is that Pune's dense student and professional population means there's rarely a shortage of things happening; the harder part is usually finding events where you're actually likely to meet people who are single and interested in meeting someone new, rather than a general public event where that's beside the point.

Where meetups already happen in Pune

A lot of Pune's informal "singles scene" runs through hobby groups rather than anything explicitly branded as a dating event. Weekend trekking groups heading out to the Sahyadris, board game and quiz nights at cafes in Koregaon Park and Baner, book clubs, and tech meetups tied to the city's IT corridor all function as de facto places to meet people — you're just there for the hobby first. Pune's college circuit also runs a constant stream of fests and cultural events that skew young and social.

The catch with most of these is discovery: unless you already know someone in the group, it's genuinely hard to find out these things exist, let alone whether the people attending are single or looking to meet someone.

It turns "is anyone else going to be single and interesting at this thing" from a guess into something you can actually see before you show up.

Tips for meeting people at events in Pune

  • Pick smaller, recurring groups over one-off big events. A weekly trekking group or quiz night gives you more natural chances to talk to the same people again, which builds familiarity faster than a huge one-time mixer.
  • Go with a specific interest, not just "to meet people." Events built around a shared hobby give you an instant conversation topic and filter out people who don't share your interests anyway.
  • Use the app to scope the vibe first. Checking who else is attending and what they're into beforehand takes some of the awkwardness out of showing up alone.
